# The Overview Effect

A grounding checklist for when things feel large and you feel small in the
wrong way. Not tasks to finish — prompts to look up from. Check one off when
you've actually let it land, not just read it.

## Widen the frame
- [ ] Name one thing visible from where you sit that is older than you
- [ ] Find the horizon — actual or implied — and rest your eyes on it
- [ ] Recall someone, anyone, doing this exact small thing somewhere else right now

## Shrink the emergency
- [ ] Say the thing you're worried about out loud, once
- [ ] Ask whether it will matter in — count them — 5 years
- [ ] Notice your breathing without changing it for three breaths — 3 breaths

## Carry it back
- [ ] Pick one small thing you can affect today
- [ ] Do that one thing — 1 thing
- [ ] Leave the rest where it is, on purpose
