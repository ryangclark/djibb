# The Word Doing the Work

Every argument has a load-bearing word. Sometimes it's the right one and it holds. Sometimes it's a flattering one that you reached for because it felt true, and it carries weight it can't actually bear. The failure is quiet — the conclusion can even be correct — but the reason is hollow, and hollow reasons collapse the moment someone leans on them. This is the check I run before I let a word decide.

## Find the load-bearing word
- [ ] Name the one word that, if false, takes the whole argument with it
      Most sentences have a passenger and a load-bearer. Point at the load-bearer on purpose, before someone else does.
- [ ] Ask whether that word actually separates the options
      I said "open." Both were the same license. A word that's equally true of every choice isn't deciding anything — it's decorating.
- [ ] Tell the flattering reason apart from the deciding one
      The flattering reason is the one that makes you sound principled. The deciding reason is the one that's still true when no one's listening.

## Tell a bet from a principle
- [ ] Sort each reason into "bet on today" or "true regardless"
      "It's more popular" is a bet on the present. "It commits the consumer to less" is a property that holds whoever wins. Don't let the first wear the second's clothes.
- [ ] Give every bet an expiry, in the same breath you make it
      A bet with no stated trigger isn't a decision, it's a habit waiting to be mistaken for one. Write the condition that would flip it.
- [ ] If the reason survives the choice's reversal, it was never your reason
      Swap the winner for the loser. If the sentence still sounds wise, it was filler. Cut it and find the one that breaks.

## Hold it honestly
- [ ] When someone cracks your reason, check if the decision survives on a better one
      It often does — and that's the good outcome, not the embarrassing one. Concede the word, keep the call, upgrade the why.
- [ ] Defend on the axis that differs, never the one that comforts
      The comforting axis is where the applause is. The differing axis is where the truth is. Stand on the truth even when it's the smaller stage.
- [ ] Leave the bet and its expiry where the next hand will find them
      A reason you sharpened in private re-dulls in public. Spend the line that records what you'd need to see to change your mind.
