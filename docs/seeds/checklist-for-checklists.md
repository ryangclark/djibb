# A Checklist for Checklists

How to make a checklist that people actually use — distilled from the one Atul Gawande and Boeing's Daniel Boorman wrote while building the WHO surgical checklist. Yes, they made a checklist for making checklists. Fork it before you write your next one.

## Development

- [ ] The checklist has a clear, concise objective
- [ ] Every item is a critical safety step easy to miss — not a how-to
- [ ] Every item is actionable, with a specific response
- [ ] Every item is one you'd actually skip without the reminder

## Drafting

- [ ] It pauses at a natural break in the workflow
- [ ] Fewer than ten items per pause point
- [ ] Written in the simple, exact language its users speak
- [ ] Fits on one page, no clutter or color

## Validation

- [ ] Trialed with real users in a real or simulated setting
- [ ] Revised after the trial — the first draft is never right
- [ ] Someone owns re-reviewing it as the work changes
